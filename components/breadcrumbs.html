Breadcrumbs

Page location indicators used at the top of every inner page, with the dashboard page-head pattern.

Default breadcrumb

Wrapped in <nav aria-label="Breadcrumb"> with an ordered list, as recommended by the WAI-ARIA authoring practices.

  1. Dashboard
  2. Applications
  3. CRM
  4. Customers
  1. Home
  2. Business
  3. Invoices

Usage notes

  • The separator is drawn by Bootstrap's --bs-breadcrumb-divider, which Qevora sets to a muted token and mirrors automatically in RTL.
  • Keep the trail to four levels or fewer; deeper hierarchies are easier to navigate from the sidebar.
  • Only the final item gets .active and aria-current="page".
  • On the dashboard page the first crumb points at index.html; in nested folders prefix it with ../.
