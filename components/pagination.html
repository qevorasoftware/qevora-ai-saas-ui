Usage notes

  • Mark the current page with .active on the .page-item and aria-current="page" on its link.
  • Prev/next controls are icon-only; give them an aria-label that names the action, not "arrow".
  • The table footer needs data-paginate="#tableId" plus a data-page-info element to receive the summary text.
  • Collapse long ranges with a disabled ellipsis item rather than truncating the numbers.
  • Always show totals ("Showing 1–3 of 128") — users need to know how much data exists behind the page.
