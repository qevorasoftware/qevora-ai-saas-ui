Privacy Policy

What personal information Qevora collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the control you keep over it.

Read the terms
Last updated: 1 October 2026 Version 3.1 · Covers the web app, the AI features and the public website. privacy@example.com

The short version

  • We collect the details you give us, the activity needed to run the workspace, and — only with your consent — usage analytics.
  • Your prompts and files are used to answer your request, never to train models for other customers.
  • We share data with the processors that run the service for us, and never sell it.
  • You can export, correct or delete your information from the workspace at any time.

1. Overview and controller

Qevora Software, Inc., 548 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94104, United States, is the controller of the personal information described here. When you use a workspace that belongs to your employer, that company is the controller of the customer data inside it and we are its processor — this policy still describes what we do with it.

This policy covers the Qevora web app, the AI features, the marketing site and support conversations. It does not cover services we link to that we do not run.

2. Information we collect

We collect three kinds of information.

  • Details you give us — name, work email, company, role, billing address and payment method, the contents of support tickets, and anything you type into a form.
  • Information created by using the service — the records you store (customers, projects, invoices, tasks), the prompts and files you submit to the AI features, the outputs they return, your settings, and the audit events that note who changed what.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser and device type, the pages you open, session timestamps and error reports. It is used to keep the service working and secure.

We do not ask for special-category data (health, beliefs, biometrics) and the workspace is not built to store it. Please do not upload it.

3. How we use information

  • to create and secure your account, and to give you the features you asked for;
  • to send the prompts you submit to the AI model you selected and return the answer to you;
  • to bill you, prevent fraud and meet our accounting duties;
  • to answer support requests and, with your consent, to follow up on them;
  • to keep the service reliable — monitoring, debugging, capacity planning;
  • to tell you about changes, new features and security notices; marketing email only where you opted in, and every message has a one-click unsubscribe.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (running the service and billing), our legitimate interests (security, product improvement, fraud prevention), your consent (optional analytics and marketing email), and legal obligation (tax and accounting records). You can withdraw consent at any time; the withdrawal does not affect what was done before it.

5. Cookies and local storage

The workspace needs a small number of cookies and local-storage entries to work: your signed-in session, the theme you picked (light or dark), the sidebar state and your text direction. These are strictly necessary and are not used for advertising.

Optional product analytics only run after you accept them, and you can change that choice from the settings page. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on any page of the product.

6. AI prompts, files and outputs

When you use an AI feature, the prompt, the files you attach and the parameters you set are sent to the model provider that serves the model you chose, so it can generate a response. That is the only reason they leave our systems.

  • Your prompts and outputs are not used to train models for other customers.
  • Model providers process the request under our agreement with them and delete it according to their retention terms, which we publish in the provider list.
  • You can switch a provider off for a workspace, or keep a prompt out of history, from the AI settings.
  • Do not paste secrets or other people's personal data into a prompt unless your workspace is allowed to process it.

7. When we share information

We never sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • Processors — cloud hosting, payment processing, email delivery, error monitoring and the AI model providers you select. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement.
  • Your own organisation — workspace owners and administrators can see the members, roles and audit trail of their workspace.
  • Authorities — when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers and the public, and then only to the extent required.
  • A successor — if the company is merged or sold, in which case you will hear about it before your data moves.

8. International transfers

Our servers are in the United States and the European Union. When personal data moves between them, or to a processor in another country, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where relevant, the UK Addendum, together with an assessment of the local law. You can ask for a copy of those safeguards.

9. How long we keep information

  • Workspace content — while your subscription is active, and for 90 days after the workspace is closed, so a cancellation can be undone and exports completed.
  • Backups — rolling 30 days, after which they are overwritten.
  • Invoices and tax records — as long as the law requires, usually seven years.
  • Support conversations and audit events — 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.

10. How we protect information

Traffic is encrypted with TLS, stored data is encrypted at rest, access to production is limited, logged and reviewed, and we run dependency and vulnerability checks on every release. Our team works on the principle of least privilege. No system is perfect, so we also keep an incident process: if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the relevant authority within the deadlines the law sets.

11. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • see a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and receive it in a portable format;
  • correct anything inaccurate, and ask us to delete information we no longer need;
  • object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent for analytics or marketing;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information as those words are used by the CCPA/CPRA — we do neither, so there is nothing to opt out of;
  • complain to your local supervisory authority. We would rather you came to us first.

Most of these you can do yourself in the workspace — export, correct or delete records from the page that holds them. For anything else, write to privacy@example.com; we answer within 30 days.

12. Children's privacy

The service is built for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and we delete it if we learn we have.

13. Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least once a year and update it when our processing changes. Material changes are announced in the workspace and by email before they take effect, and the date at the top of the page always shows the version in force.

14. Contact us

Privacy questions and requests go to privacy@example.com, or by post to Qevora Software, Inc., Attn: Data Protection Officer, 548 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94104, United States. Our EU representative can be reached at the same address from the European Union and the United Kingdom.
