Usage notes

  • Tooltips are initialised from [data-bs-toggle="tooltip"] in app.js — no per-page script needed.
  • Always keep a real aria-label on icon-only triggers: tooltips are not announced reliably.
  • Never put interactive content (links, buttons) inside a tooltip — use a popover with data-bs-trigger="focus".
  • Keep titles under about 40 characters and bodies under two short sentences.
  • Tooltips follow the theme automatically because they sit on the surface tokens, including in dark mode.
