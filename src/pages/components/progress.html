Usage notes

  • Every progress bar needs role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax.
  • Show the number as text next to the bar — a bar alone cannot tell the user whether 68% is good or bad.
  • Reserve amber for "close to a limit" and red for "at or over a limit" so the colours keep their meaning.
  • Rings update on theme change because they are drawn from the same colour tokens as the charts.
  • Counters respect prefers-reduced-motion: the final value is written immediately for those users.
