Copying a component

<!-- 1. Open any component page and switch to the HTML tab -->
<!-- 2. Press "Copy HTML" -->

<div class="alert alert-success d-flex gap-2" role="alert">
  <i class="bi bi-check-circle alert-icon"></i>
  <div>
    <strong class="d-block">Workspace updated</strong>
    Your changes are saved.
  </div>
</div>

<!-- 3. Paste it into your page — the styles already exist -->
Components rely only on bootstrap.min.css plus Qevora's own style.css and components.css. If the stylesheets are already linked, any pasted block renders correctly in light and dark mode.
