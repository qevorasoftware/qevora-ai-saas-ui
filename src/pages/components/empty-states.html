Empty, error and loading states

Every list, table and chart in the template has a defined state for “nothing here yet”, “something broke” and “still loading”.

Empty state

Explain what is missing, why, and the one action that fixes it. Used by tables, kanban boards and AI history.

Error and search states

Distinguish “no results for this filter” from “this failed”. The recovery action differs for each.

No results found

No invoices match “Orbit”. Try a different keyword or clear the filters.

Could not load analytics

The reporting service did not respond. Your data is safe — try again in a moment.
