Usage notes

  • Always give the dialog aria-labelledby pointing at the visible .modal-title and an aria-label on the close button.
  • Add data-bs-backdrop="static" only for destructive confirmations — users must not lose the dialog by accident.
  • Keep footers to two buttons: a white or ghost cancel on the left, one primary action on the right.
  • Use .modal-dialog-scrollable for logs and activity lists instead of inventing a custom maximum height.
  • Focus is handled by Bootstrap: the first focusable element receives it on open and returns on close.
