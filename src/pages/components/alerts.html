Alerts

Inline feedback for success, warning, error and information states, with an optional dismiss button.

Soft alerts

The default style. Each alert pairs an icon with a title and a short explanation.

Payment captured Invoice INV-2026-0184 was paid in full.
AI quota almost reached Nova-4 Turbo is at 82% of its monthly limit.
Payment failed The card ending 1009 was declined by the issuer.
Scheduled maintenance Reporting exports pause on Oct 8 between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.
New component library 31 component pages with copy-ready markup are ready to browse. 
<div class="alert alert-success" role="alert">
  <i class="bi bi-check-circle alert-icon"></i>
  <div>
    <strong class="d-block">Payment captured</strong>
    <span class="fs-8">Invoice INV-2026-0184 was paid in full.</span>
  </div>
</div>

Dismissible and solid

Dismissible alerts include a close button with an accessible label. Solid alerts are reserved for high-emphasis page-level messages.

Your export is being prepared. We will email you when it is ready.
Settings saved. Your workspace preferences are up to date.
Qevora v1.1 ships next month with new AI modules.
Trial ends in three days for Acme Inc.
<div class="alert alert-info alert-dismissible fade show" role="alert">
  <i class="bi bi-info-circle alert-icon"></i>
  <div class="flex-grow-1">Your export is being prepared.</div>
  <button type="button" class="btn-close" data-bs-dismiss="alert" aria-label="Dismiss notification"></button>
</div>

<div class="alert alert-solid-primary" role="alert">
  <i class="bi bi-megaphone alert-icon"></i>
  <div class="flex-grow-1">Qevora v1.1 ships next month.</div>
</div>

Usage notes

  • Every alert uses role="alert" so assistive technology announces it when inserted.
  • The icon reflects the meaning, not just the colour — pair the two for accessibility.
  • Keep alert copy to two lines. Longer explanations belong in a modal or documentation.
  • Set aria-live="polite" on a container when you inject alerts dynamically.
