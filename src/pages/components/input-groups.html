Usage notes

  • Wrap the field and its add-ons in one .input-group; never place a group inside another group.
  • .input-group-sm and -lg must be applied to the group, not to the input, so the add-ons match the height.
  • Give bare inputs inside a group an aria-label when their visible label only covers the first field.
  • Buttons inside groups keep the btn radius rules — use .btn-soft-* to keep the group quiet.
  • Do not mix a .form-floating wrapper with an input group; choose one pattern per field.
