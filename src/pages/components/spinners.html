Usage notes

  • Every spinner needs role="status" and a .visually-hidden label so screen readers announce the wait.
  • Keep indicators under about one second of perceived delay; anything longer deserves a skeleton or a progress bar.
  • Disable the triggering button while it is loading, or users will submit the same action twice.
  • Match the spinner colour to the button it sits in — .spinner-border-sm plus the button's own text colour works everywhere.
  • Never stack a spinner on top of stale data; either keep the old rows visible with a subtle overlay, or show skeletons.
