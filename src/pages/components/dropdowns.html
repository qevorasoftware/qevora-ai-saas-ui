Usage notes

  • Every dropdown needs data-bs-toggle="dropdown" on the trigger and .dropdown-menu as the next sibling.
  • Add .dropdown-menu-end for right-aligned menus — required in table action columns so the menu stays on screen.
  • Put row actions in a .btn-icon trigger with an aria-label; text triggers are for page-level actions only.
  • Icons inside items are decorative — the item label always carries the meaning.
  • Dropdowns close on Esc out of the box; keep destructive actions in the last group, after a divider.
